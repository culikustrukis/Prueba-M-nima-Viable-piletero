## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a crystalline, professional, and refreshing digital presence for premium residential and commercial pool maintenance. The brand personality balances pristine technical reliability with sensory tranquility—conveying the feeling of clear, hygienic, sunlit water and effortless home service. 

The aesthetic is Modern Tactile Minimalism with subtle aqueous glass elements. It avoids gimmicky aquatic clip-art in favor of high-precision typography, luminous cyan accents, architectural whitespace, and surgical card surfaces that reflect sanitation standards, transparency, and top-tier hospitality.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in aquatic clarity and clinical hygiene.

- **Primary (`#0077B6`) & Deep Water Navy (`#023E8A`)**: Express authority, depth, and enterprise dependability. Used for primary CTAs, primary navigation links, active states, and commanding headlines.
- **Secondary Turquoise (`#00B4D8`) & Sky Aqua (`#48CAE4`)**: Represent sunlight refracting through fresh pool water. Used for micro-badges, key highlights, hover transitions, and service tier distinctions.
- **Tertiary Mint Accent (`#2DD4BF`)**: Evokes purification, eco-friendly water treatment, and pristine chlorine balance. Used selectively for confirmation toasts, certified badges, and status pills.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC` to `#F1F5F9`)**: A cool pearl-gray foundation that prevents harsh glare while ensuring crisp contrast against pure white card surfaces (`#FFFFFF`). Text defaults to an ink slate (`#0F172A`) to ensure WCAG AAA legibility.

## Typography

The type scale combines **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headlines with **Inter** for data and functional body copy.

- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides a geometric, buoyant energy with rounded terminal precision, giving headings an open, approachable, and premium tone.
- **Inter** handles high-density booking forms, chemical reading metrics, pricing tables, and address scheduling details with zero distortion and neutral clarity.
- Headlines maintain tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.015em` to `-0.03em`) to anchor authority on large display viewports.
- Body text remains uncompressed with generous line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to maximize readability in outdoor and bright lighting conditions.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a flexible 12-column grid system bounded at an inner max-width of `1280px` for desktop, collapsing smoothly to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile viewports.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns, `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters, and `2rem` (`32px`) external margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns, `1.25rem` (`20px`) gutters, and `1.5rem` (`24px`) external margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` (`16px`) gutters, and `1rem` (`16px`) external canvas padding.
- Spacing follows a 4px/8px modular cadence. Booking flow funnels restrict horizontal width to `640px` centered to reduce cognitive load during appointment scheduling.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth mirrors the ambient optics of clear water: light, layered, and translucent without heavy muddiness.

- **Surface Layers**: Canvas sits at `#F8FAFC`. Base cards are pure white (`#FFFFFF`) framed with an ultra-fine border (`1px solid #E2E8F0`).
- **Ambient Hydro-Shadows**: Elevation abandons harsh black drops in favor of tinted deep-navy shadows (`rgba(2, 62, 138, 0.06)` for rests, increasing to `rgba(0, 119, 182, 0.12)` for interactive hovers).
- **Glassmorphism**: Sticky navigation bars and floating checkout summaries utilize `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` over `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with a `1px` translucent border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`), evoking sunlight skimming surface water.
- **Focus Rings**: Emphasized with an outer water-glow (`0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.35)`).

## Shapes

The shape hierarchy is unified under Level 2 (Rounded):

- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs, Selects)**: Built with `0.5rem` (`8px`) radii for a friendly yet structured touch target.
- **Cards & Modal Containers**: Styled with `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / `16px`) to produce soft, contained modules that feel modern and approachable.
- **Hero Banners & Visual Panels**: Utilize `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / `24px`) to establish distinct content zones.
- **Badges, Status Chips, & Filter Pills**: Fully rounded pill shapes (`9999px`) to emphasize fluidity and frictionless state switching.

## Components

### Buttons & Action Triggers
- **Primary Action (Agendar Servicio / Pedir Visita)**: Deep pool blue (`#0077B6`) solid background, `#FFFFFF` text, `font-weight: 600`, subtle inset highlight on top edge (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.25)`). Hover elevates with `#023E8A` and an aqua-tinted glow.
- **Secondary Action**: Bordered button with `1.5px solid #00B4D8`, transparent background, text color `#0077B6`. Transitions to an ice-cyan fill (`#E0F2FE`) on hover.
- **Floating Mobile CTA**: Persistent fixed dock on mobile containing service price summary and a full-width instant booking trigger.

### Input Fields & Booking Forms
- **Fields**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, internal padding `0.75rem 1rem`. 
- **Focus State**: Border color snaps to `#0077B6` with an offset cyan halo (`0 0 0 3px rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.25)`).
- **Date & Slot Pickers**: Clean grid blocks; active slots highlight with `#0077B6` background and pure white text.

### Cards & Service Tiers
- **Service Cards**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, padded with `1.5rem`. Hover state creates a slight translateY (`-3px`) and brings up an ambient shadow tinted with `#0077B6`.
- **Selected Plan Card**: Outlined in `2px solid #00B4D8` with a tiny top badge labeled "Recomendado" using the mint tertiary tone (`#2DD4BF`).

### Chips, Pills & Tags
- **Status Indicators (Piscina Lista, En Ruta, En Espera)**: Light background tint (`rgba(45, 212, 191, 0.15)` for mint, `rgba(0, 180, 216, 0.15)` for cyan) paired with a solid dot indicator and dark text.
- **Filter Chips**: Pill shape, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, turns solid `#023E8A` when active.

### Checkboxes & Radio Elements
- Custom circular indicators for radio (pool dimensions, water condition) and rounded squares (`4px`) for checkboxes (additional services: control de pH, aspirado de fondo, limpieza de filtros). Active states fill with `#0077B6` showing a crisp white glyph.

### Diagnostic & Inspection Water Metrics (Specialized Component)
- Compact data metric card showing pool health indicators (pH level, Chlorine ppm, Alkalinity). Features a horizontal micro-gauge bar colored from deep aqua to fresh mint indicating optimal balance ranges.
